## Where I've Built Things

**[NTT DATA Europe & Latam](https://www.nttdata.com/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Senior Product Designer | Product Strategy &nbsp;·&nbsp; *2025 – present*\
Design system for one of Brazil's largest financial institutions. Governance, scalability, WCAG AA accessibility. Acting at the intersection of product, design, and technology — contributing to OKRs, roadmap alignment, and stakeholder strategy in an agile environment.

**[Globalweb Corp](https://www.globalweb.com.br/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Senior Product Designer | Product Strategy & Discovery &nbsp;·&nbsp; *2022 – 2025*\
Led complex product redesigns at massive scale for government and financial sector clients, including a project impacting tens of millions of users and a regulated financial platform with Central Bank compliance requirements. End-to-end ownership: research, journey mapping, prototyping, design system implementation, and agile delivery.

**[Indra](https://www.indracompany.com/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Senior Designer &nbsp;·&nbsp; *2014 – 2022*\
8+ years in an international team reporting directly to headquarters in Madrid. Led strategic digital projects, managed high-pressure stakeholder relationships, and drove product decisions across design, technology, and business.

**[Sanrio Brasil](https://www.sanrio.com.br/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Senior Product Designer &nbsp;·&nbsp; *2009 – 2013*\
Launched **Brazil's first Hello Kitty e-commerce**, including full stack coordination: market research, pricing, logistics, Magento dev, payment integration, and analytics. Created the Hello Kitty app for LG Smart TVs and contributed to the first Hello Kitty smartphone design.
