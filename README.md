Fifteen years in digital products taught me one thing: the hardest part is never the interface. It's deciding what to build, aligning people around it, and knowing when to stop.

My background spans design, development, and strategy. That context shapes how I talk to engineers, challenge assumptions in discovery, and use AI as a tool without losing track of the actual problem.
